FIFTH WEEK
Each week, each group is required to present their project.   Show your tutor:
 
· What (you are delivering);
· When (you will be delivering);
· Why (you have recommended the artefacts you are delivering);
· Who (on your team is building what);
· How much (time you plan to spend on your project and time you have spent on your project.  What is the gap and what are you doing about it)
· Top risks last week
· Major changes last week  (including scope)
· Key issues last week 
Presentation Properties
Use models rather than words.
 
Your tutors are looking for the ‘big picture’ that describes your project.  Be prepared to answer with detail at question time.


This week’s specials
YYour team must deliver an overview of your mobile application design, showing both:
* how your application architecture has evolved, and

* what you plan to complete by the end of the semester.

Specifically, you must present:
1. The Models Used to Design & Manage Your Mobile App
Provide visual models, such as:
   * App Architecture Diagram (MVVM / MVP / Flutter Architecture / SwiftUI flow)

   * Navigation Flow Diagram

   * API Interaction / Backend Communication Diagram

   * Database / Local Storage Schema

   * UI/UX Wireframes or Component Tree

   * Sprint or Kanban Board structure (if used for planning)

These models should clearly explain how the app works end-to-end.

2. Quality Measures Used for Each Model
Describe the quality frameworks and checks applied, such as:
      * Code Quality Measures

         * Clean Architecture principles

         * SOLID, DRY, KISS

         * Linting & static analysis

         * Modularization

            * UI/UX Quality Measures

               * Consistency rules

               * Accessibility guidelines (contrast, tap target sizes, etc.)

               * Material Design / Human Interface Guidelines compliance

                  * Performance Measures

                     * Frame rate performance

                     * Memory usage

                     * Network efficiency

                     * Battery impact analysis

                        * Testing Measures

                           * Unit tests (ViewModel, controllers, services)

                           * UI tests

                           * Integration tests

                           * Manual test cases / QA checklist

Explain how you know your mobile app is working well.


Goal of Week 5
By the end of this week, the team should demonstrate:
                              * A coherent, evolving architecture

                              * Clear modeling of UI, data, and logic layers

                              * Strong quality-control practices

                              * A working prototype aligned with design principles

                              * A roadmap to complete the final version of the app
